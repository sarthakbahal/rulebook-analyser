# Fee Deadlines

> **Corpus status:** The fee schedule below is synthetic test data created to accompany the handbook corpus. The uploaded Mind and Hand Book is a conduct-and-policy document and does not establish these dollar amounts. Use this file only for the benchmark.

## Table 1. Tuition, Hostel, and Late-Fee Schedule

| Charge | Amount | Standard deadline | Late-payment penalty | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Undergraduate tuition | $31,250 per semester | 15 August (Fall) / 10 January (Spring) | $100 after 5 calendar days | Payment is due by the posted term deadline. |
| Graduate tuition | $33,500 per semester | 15 August (Fall) / 10 January (Spring) | $100 after 5 calendar days | Applies to the standard test-corpus graduate schedule. |
| Hostel / residence fee | $8,400 per semester | 15 August (Fall) / 10 January (Spring) | $75 after 3 calendar days | Covers the synthetic residential charge used in this dataset. |
| Meal-plan charge | $2,750 per semester | 15 August (Fall) / 10 January (Spring) | $50 after 5 calendar days | Synthetic benchmark value. |
| Library overdue fee | $2 per day per item | When the item is due | Not applicable | The charge is assessed for each calendar day overdue. |

### Payment procedure

Students should review their account before the deadline and retain proof of payment. A payment made after the stated deadline can trigger the corresponding late charge. The synthetic schedule does not specify installment plans, scholarship timing, tax treatment, payment methods, or appeals.

## Table 2. Library Deposits and Refund Deadlines

| Item | Amount | Claim deadline / rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Library caution deposit | $500 | **Library caution deposit refund must be claimed within 30 days of graduation.** |
| Library overdue item | $2/day/item | Charged for each calendar day after the due date. |
| Replacement processing charge | $35/item | Applied when an unreturned item requires replacement processing. |

### Library clearance

Students should return outstanding library materials and complete any required library clearance before final departure. A refund request should include the student's identification details and evidence of cleared obligations as required by the synthetic test procedure.

### Benchmark note

The 30-day library refund deadline in Table 2 conflicts with a statement inserted into page 18 of `academic_handbook.pdf`: **"Library security clearance and refund claims remain valid for up to 1 year post-graduation."** The contradiction is deliberate and is documented in `contradictions.md`.

## Quick deadline reference

- Tuition: 15 August for Fall; 10 January for Spring.
- Hostel: 15 August for Fall; 10 January for Spring.
- Tuition late fee: $100 after 5 calendar days.
- Hostel late fee: $75 after 3 calendar days.
- Library overdue fee: $2 per day per item.
- Library caution deposit refund: within 30 days of graduation.
